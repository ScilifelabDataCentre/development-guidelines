# Regulations

This is not a comprehensive document of regulatory requirements, and further requirements might apply to specific applications. The following are some of the most important regulations which we need to consider when developing software:

- [General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)](#general-data-protection-regulation-gdpr)
- [Web Accessibility Directive (WAD)](#web-accessibility-directive-wad)
- [Regulations for state agencies/statliga myndigheter](#regulations-for-state-agenciesstatliga-myndigheter)

## General Data Protection Regulation: GDPR

The GDPR is the EU regulation on how personal data may be collected and processed. The following links contain useful information:

- [A developer's guide to GDPR (IBM)](https://developer.ibm.com/articles/s-gdpr1/)
- [Processing personal data (verksamt.se)](https://verksamt.se/en/agreement-invoicing/personal-data)
- [6 steps to comply with GDPR (IMY)](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/innovationsportalen/ar-det-ni-tankt-gora-ar-forenligt-med-gdpr/) [^1]

Three points to follow:

> - Do not collect more personal data than is necessary and only for a specific, predetermined purpose.
> - Inform about what data you date and to not save it longer than necessary.
> - Protect the personal data you handle in the company.
>
> _Source: [verksamt.se](https://verksamt.se/en/agreement-invoicing/personal-data)_

[^1]: IMY stands for Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, the Swedish authority on privacy protection

## Web Accessibility Directive: WAD

The [WAD](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/web-accessibility) requires public-sector (incl. universities) websites and mobile apps to be accessible.
In Sweden, it is implemented through ["Lagen om tillgänglighet till digital offentlig service"](https://svenskforfattningssamling.se/sites/default/files/sfs/2018-11/SFS2018-1937.pdf) (DOS-lagen, the Act on Accessibility to Digital Public Services).

The WAD complements the [European Accessibility Act (EAA)](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/european-accessibility-act-eaa_en),
which covers specific products and services, also in the private sector.

See [Accessibility docs](../accessibility/) for details on how to make your service accessible.

## Regulations for state agencies/statliga myndigheter

Universities are state agencies and thus Swedish Civil Contingencies Agency’s (MSB) directives apply by law:

- Information security in governmental authorities ([MSBFS 2020:6](https://www.mcf.se/sv/regler/gallande-regler/msbfs-20206/))
- Security measures in information systems for governmental authorities ([MSBFS 2020:7](https://www.mcf.se/sv/regler/gallande-regler/msbfs-20207/))
- Reporting IT incidents for governmental authorities ([MSBFS 2020:8](https://www.mcf.se/sv/regler/gallande-regler/foreskrifter-om-rapportering-av-it-incidenter-for-statliga-myndigheter-msbfs-20208/)).

Each university then uses these to derive their own concrete procedures, for example [Uppsala University](https://www.uu.se/medarbetare/organisation-styrning/regler/riktlinjer-for-systematiskt-informationssakerhetsarbete). As they are all derived from the same parent directives, the fundamentals should be the same. These derived university guidelines are more succinct and concrete than the MSB documents.
