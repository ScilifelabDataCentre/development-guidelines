# Roadmap process

This is both a process and a [document template](.).

A roadmap is a high-level document (always <10 pages, usually shorter, can be 1 page) which lays out the key goals for a
product/service over a specific time period.

## When do you use this process/template?

On a recurring basis, typically once a year, for a service that exists or will exist.

## Why?

- Goal alignment & shared high-level vision for all key stakeholders
- Effective resource allocation (esp. between products)
- Risk mitigation

### Relationship with teams' autonomy

- Teams are autonomous in their day-to-day/week-to-week
- To maintain that autonomy, there must be a written high-level vision to ensure there is a shared vision.
- Not in opposition to agile processes because roadmaps do not contain a very detailed description of 'how' and contain
  some info that backlogs usually do not

1. A service already exists or the proposal for a new service has been signed off
2. Product/Service Owner leads the planning, with involvement from Responsible, Accountable, and ¨
   likely, Consulted
3. Write the proposal (using a copy of the [template](.)) and place it in the `In Review` folder for the appropriate year (e.g. [2025 > In Review](.))
4. [DCMG](glossary.md#dcmg-) reviews it
5. Once the roadmap is signed off, move it to the Approved folder for the appropriate year

> [!NOTE]  
> Steps 3-4 are an iterative loop with feedback and changes.

## Tips

- The roadmap is a living document - if the plans change, or need to change (e.g. team size changes), substantially, the
  roadmap should be updated. If there is high uncertainty around a service, a shorter period roadmap (e.g. 3 months)
  might be more appropriate

> [!WARNING]
> If the roadmap requires very frequent, unplanned updates, this may indicate a problem. This could be that the roadmap
> is too detailed **or** that there is not alignment on the purpose of the service. That is an underlying issue that is
> highlighted, but cannot be solved, by the roadmap.

- Refresh your understanding of [RACI roles](raci.md).
- Be concise
- Start by reviewing the last roadmap - What can we improve? Over-optimistic? Missing resources?
