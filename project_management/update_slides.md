# Cross-team update slides

## What are they?

- A deck of [Google Slides](.) prepared at the end of every month, with a single slide for every service we develop.
- Each slide gives the headline updates for a service from the last month - see [the example for a fictional service](.).
- The target audience for your slide(s) is everyone in DC, but especially DCMG.

## Why?

- To share, in written form, the headlines about each service.
- There are too many people and products for everyone to know the details of everything, but it's important
  to share a high-level snapshot.
- Sharing information in a structured way is a key method to prevent [information silos](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-life-hacks/organization/understanding-information-silos) -
  DC's development projects share many target users, overlapping or complimentary purposes and technology stacks, so a
  high-level view of other projects is key.

## What is the process?

### If you're a team

1. When the new slides are sent towards the end of the month, update the slide(s) for your service(s). The 4 categories
   of updates are:
   - _Progress update_ - general headlines about work completed on the service in the last month
   - _Wins_ - successes that you want to highlight & celebrate: no win is too small if it's progress for that service
     that you want to celebrate.
   - _Risks_ - anything that you want to highlight that may negatively affect the service/team reaching the goals
   - _Support needed_ - when you need someone else to do something to unblock you or mitigate a risk. If there is a
     specific timeline (other than just 'as soon as possible'), include that.
2. Update the 'Updated by' box.

### If you're the person doing the admin

#### To prepare new slides...

1. At least a week before the end of the month, copy the [previous month's slide deck](.)
   and rename it to this month (i.e. _YYYY MMMM cross-team update_).
2. Move last month's slides into [Archive](.).
3. Reset the 'Updated by' text box to 'Updated by: XXXXX' on every slide - so it's clear when a slide has not been
   updated.
4. Link the slides in the `#datacentre_dev` Slack and provide people with a clear deadline to fill them out. Set a
   timed reminder a day or so before that deadline.
5. After the deadline, mark slides which have not been updated as 'Not updated', so it's clear that the notes are from
   the previous month.
6. Send a message with the slides as both a PDF and a link to the `#datacentre_internal` Slack channel.

#### Once the slides are ready...

1. Read through the slides, contacting the author if you need clarity on something.
2. For _Progress update_, _Wins_ and _Risks_, take points that need attention to [DCMG](glossary.md#dcmg-).
3. For _Support needed_, follow up each item as applicable (e.g. ask one team to prioritise something for another,
   raise the issue at the next [DCMG](glossary.md#dcmg-) meeting).

## Tips

- The slides are per product/service, _not_ per team. This is to encourage information-sharing for teams' smaller
  projects (e.g. those in maintenance) and for findability.
- It's fine to simply note that no work has been done on a product/service in the last month, but
  always update the slide.
- At least 1 person from a team should take a look at other services' slides. Have they just solved an issue that you
  also have or are they blocked by one that you have already solved?
